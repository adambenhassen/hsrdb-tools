CIFs corrected from their publications

500 COD CIFs cannot give a correct pattern as deposited, but the original article, or the CIF's own statements, say what the file leaves out or gets wrong. For these the build applies a correction after reading the CIF; the CIF files are never changed, and each entry says in its comment in HighScore that it was corrected and from which source. A correction is added only when the source itself gives the missing information, and each one is checked against the CIF's formula, Z and density.
