How to check and unpack a download

On Windows, check a download in PowerShell with Get-FileHash on the file (SHA-256 is the default) and compare the hash with SHA256SUMS.txt, or right-click the file › 7-Zip › CRC SHA › SHA-256; then unpack it with 7-Zip. On Linux or macOS: sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS.txt.

How the patterns are calculated

Diffraction model

  • Kinematic structure factors with the exact International Tables (1992) form-factor coefficients and Cromer–Liberman f′ and f″ at Cu Kα₁ (1.540598 Å).
  • |F|² averaged over Friedel pairs, so anomalous scattering is treated correctly in non-centrosymmetric structures.
  • The published displacement parameters: anisotropic U, B or β tensors, each rotated with its symmetry image; B = 0.5 Å² only where a CIF gives none, as HighScore and the official databases do.
  • Powder Lorentz–polarisation (1 + cos²2θ)/(sin²θ cos θ); lines to 90° 2θ, merged within 0.005°, at most 203 lines, scaled to 1000.
  • Reflections that fall at the same angle without being symmetry-equivalent are added, as a measured line contains all of them.

Structures as published

  • Symmetry from the CIF's listed operations, else its Hall symbol, else its Hermann–Mauguin symbol; non-standard settings, shifted origins and supercells standardised with spglib only when it finds exactly the listed group.
  • Stored in the reference setting HighScore expects, in the same cell as the official databases (reduced monoclinic and triclinic cells, conventional orthorhombic axes).
  • Split atoms next to special positions kept or merged by the CIF's own site symmetry, occupancies and density; dummy atoms (ring centroids) do not scatter.
  • Modulated structures calculated from their superspace model, with satellites; the result is checked against the structure factors the refinement program lists in the CIF.
  • 793 CIFs completed or corrected, each cited and marked in the entry: 500 from their original publications (or from the CIF's own statements), and 293 whose transcribed structure contradicts itself, with a corrected structure of the same publication; the CIF files themselves are never changed.

Every entry carries the cell, space group, unit-cell content, calculated density, I/Ic, the structure record (atoms, occupancies, displacement parameters, Wyckoff positions) and the literature reference, and belongs to the inorganic, organic, mineral and metallic subfiles as in the official databases.

Verification

Against cctbx

Every pattern of COD was recalculated with cctbx, an independent crystallographic library, in two ways: from the structure as read here (structure factors, form and displacement factors, multiplicities, Lorentz–polarisation), and from cctbx's own reading of the raw CIF (parsing, settings, site handling). The full run gave no calculation differences; each reading difference has a documented cause, such as cctbx treating dummy atoms as scatterers. The 576 entries this release changes were checked again (structure factors and patterns exactly as cctbx computes them), and so was the finished database.

Using it in HighScore

  1. Download the archive for your HighScore version and check its SHA-256.
  2. Unpack it with 7-Zip; keep the .hsrdb on a local disk.
  3. Add the file as a reference database in HighScore.
  4. Untick or remove other COD databases first: they use the same 96- reference codes, and HighScore refuses a second database with conflicting prefixes.

Reference codes follow the official scheme, 96- + (COD id + 1): COD 9009599 is 96-900-9600. Each entry links back to its COD page through its id.

What is not included

1,228 CIFs cannot give a pattern: 1,017 have no atom coordinates, 152 no symmetry, 46 no unit cell, and a few have symmetry or atom types that cannot be read without guessing. None of them is in the official COD 2024 database either. Each was checked; where the original article gives what the CIF lacks, the entry is built from it.

2026-10-31 533,950 entries

  • Atom rows that a CIF lists again as symmetry images of another row (for example every atom of the cell listed besides the space group) count once where counting them separately contradicts the CIF's own formula or density, or gives an impossible density (303 entries); the entry's comment names them. Before, the Wyckoff-compilation CIFs reached up to 70 g/cm³.
  • Negative occupancies are read as 0, and the entry's comment says so (11 entries; most are refined zeros).
  • 293 CIFs whose transcribed structure contradicts itself (atoms on top of each other, or a unit-cell content against the CIF's own formula) are corrected: 32 Wyckoff-compilation CIFs read in origin choice 1, and 261 with a corrected structure of the same publication, not checked against the articles. They are listed on the corrections page (793 corrected CIFs in all).
  • Built from the same COD mirror as 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30.

2026-10-30 533,950 entries

  • Deuterium has its own element flag, as in the official databases: a HighScore element search for D finds the 1,760 deuterated entries (before, D shared hydrogen's flag).
  • Occupancies of 1.5 or more, which no site can hold (typing errors such as 130 for 0.130, or ratios such as 2 and 3), are read as 1, as in the official COD 2024 database, and the entry's comment says so (219 entries). Before, they gave too many atoms, up to impossible densities of 115 g/cm³.
  • Built from the same COD mirror as 2026-10-29.

2026-10-29 533,950 entries

  • 11 CIFs added to the COD since the previous release give entries (22,367 newer than the official COD 2024 database); one more unpublished deposit, without coordinates, is skipped (1,228 in all).
  • The COD revised seven CIFs in the meantime (1010542, 4100872–4100877); the entries 4100872–4100877 now carry their correct authors, title, journal and pages.
  • A calculation error in four commensurately modulated structures is fixed (COD 2310038, 2310043, 2310046, 2310055): line intensities change by up to 5 on the 0–1000 scale, I/Ic by up to 1.4%.
  • A line holding several reflections of equal intensity at one d-spacing now carries the same hkl label on every platform (2,849 entries); patterns are unchanged.
